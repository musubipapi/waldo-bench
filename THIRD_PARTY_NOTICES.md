## IVSN Waldo dataset

Waldo Bench uses the Waldo scenes, labels, and text masks from the IVSN dataset:

Mengmi Zhang, Jiashi Feng, Keng Teck Ma, Joo Hwee Lim, Qi Zhao and Gabriel Kreiman, "Finding any Waldo
with zero-shot invariant and efficient visual search," *Nature Communications* 9, 3730 (2018).
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-06217-x

Source: https://github.com/kreimanlab/VisualSearchZeroShot (Part 1 of the datasets).
`waldobench extract` checks that the archive matches SHA-256
`01bd5a20e2f20bba5456e8bdabc4a11d00f55f445f061ac232ebd4d2cf380640`.

The dataset is covered by the Kreiman lab license agreement. It is not included in this repository
and is not relicensed by it. Download it yourself and follow its terms.
